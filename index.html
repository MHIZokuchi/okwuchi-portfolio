About Me

I'm a Senior Software Engineer with a B.Eng. from FUTO and a Master's in Computing (Distinction) from the University of Huddersfield. As Backend Team Lead at Africhange Technologies, I've been instrumental in architecting systems that enabled the platform to scale operations across the UK, Nigeria, Canada, and Australia.

My expertise spans backend architecture, fintech infrastructure, API design, and team leadership. I'm particularly passionate about building systems that handle complex financial transactions with audit trails, multi-currency support, and compliance-first design.
